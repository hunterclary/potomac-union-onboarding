Your Union Onboarding Experience

Joining Union means putting your practice, and your clients’ assets, onto a new platform. That is a big decision, and we do not expect you to figure it out on your own. Here is exactly what happens, in order, from your first conversation with our team to opening your first account.

The Roadmap
1

Initial Advisor Onboarding

Once you have decided to bring your business to Union, we start with a kickoff conversation. This is where we map out your practice, your client base, and what you need from the platform, so every step after this one is built around your specific situation instead of a generic checklist.

2

Executed Co-Advisor Agreement

Next comes the paperwork that makes the partnership official: a Co-Advisor Agreement between Potomac and your broker-dealer or RIA (the firm you are affiliated with as an advisor). Once this is signed, your firm is formally recognized as a co-advisor on the Union platform, and we can begin setting up your access.

3

Union TAMP Onboarding Call

With the agreement in place, we schedule a dedicated onboarding call focused entirely on Union, our turnkey asset management platform. This is a guided walkthrough of how the platform works day to day: model selection, billing, reporting, and the tools you will use most. Bring your questions. This call is built for them.

4

Union Platform Login

You will receive your credentials to log into Union itself, the system where you will manage models, monitor accounts, and pull reporting for your clients. Your login is your entry point to everything the platform does for you.

5

DocuSign Login

Client paperwork and account applications move through DocuSign, our e-signature system. Your DocuSign login lets you send and manage that paperwork directly, keeping the client experience smooth from the very first form.

6

Custodial Master Code

You will also receive your custodial master code, the identifier that connects new client accounts to your advisor relationship at the custodian, the institution that actually holds and safeguards your clients’ assets. This code is what ties every account you open back to you.

7

Account Opening Walkthrough

Finally, we walk through opening your first account together. This is not a manual you read on your own. A member of our team sits with you through the real process, so by the time you open your next account solo, you have already done it once with support.

That's the full path

From signed agreement to fully operational advisor on Union, every step is designed to get you moving as quickly as possible without cutting corners on the details that protect you and your clients.
